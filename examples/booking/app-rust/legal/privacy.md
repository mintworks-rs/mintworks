# Privacy Policy

_This is demonstration text for an example application. It is not legal advice and it is not
fit for use by a real business._

## 1. Who processes your data

Példa Szolgáltató Kft., 1051 Budapest, Példa utca 1., Hungary, as controller.

## 2. What we store, and why

- **Your email address and password hash** — to let you sign in. Legal basis: performance of
  the contract.
- **Your billing details** (name, address, tax number) — to issue a valid invoice. Legal
  basis: a legal obligation under Hungarian invoicing law.
- **Your bookings and invoices** — to deliver and bill the service, and to satisfy the
  statutory retention period for accounting records.
- **Your consents** — which version of which document you accepted, and when. Legal basis:
  our legitimate interest in being able to prove it.

## 3. Who else sees it

Issued invoices are reported to NAV (Nemzeti Adó- és Vámhivatal), as the law requires. Email
is delivered by our mail provider. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

## 4. How long we keep it

Accounting records are kept for the statutory retention period. Your account details and
billing address are anonymised when you close your account. Bookings are kept, linked to the
anonymised account, because they are the basis of invoices we are legally required to retain.

## 5. Your rights

You can export your account, billing and invoice data, or ask for your account to be
anonymised, from within the application. The export does not include your bookings; ask us
and we will send them to you. Consent records and issued invoices survive anonymisation,
because they are evidence we are legally required to keep.
