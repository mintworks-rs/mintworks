billed by the occasion. You book them through this application; we invoice what you booked.

You are responsible for keeping your password to yourself and for the accuracy of the billing
details you enter. Invoices are issued to the billing party recorded on your account at the
moment of issue.

## 3. Booking and cancellation

A booking is a request until it appears on a confirmed invoice. You may remove an unbilled
booking at any time. Once a booking is on an issued invoice it can only be reversed by a
storno invoice.

## 4. Invoicing and payment

Prices are shown net, in HUF, and VAT is added at the statutory rate. Issuing an invoice
allocates a number from our invoice series and reports the invoice to NAV, as Hungarian law
requires. An issued invoice is immutable: corrections are made by storno.
