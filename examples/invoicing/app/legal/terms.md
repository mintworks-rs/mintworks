## 1. The service

Példa Szolgáltató Kft. ("we") provides a back-office application for issuing invoices,
managing partners, services and projects, and filing issued invoices with NAV.

## 2. Your account

You are responsible for keeping your password to yourself and for the accuracy of the data you
enter. Invoices are issued in the name of the seller configured for the deployment, to the
partner recorded on the invoice at the moment of issue.

## 3. Invoicing

Issuing an invoice allocates a number from the invoice series and reports the invoice to NAV,
as Hungarian law requires. An issued invoice is immutable: corrections are made by storno.
