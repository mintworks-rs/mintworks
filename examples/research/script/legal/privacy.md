# Privacy Policy

_This is demonstration text for an example application. It is not legal advice and it is not
fit for use by a real business._

## 1. Who processes your data

Példa Szolgáltató Kft., 1051 Budapest, Példa utca 1., Hungary, as controller.

## 2. What we store, and why

- **Your email address and password hash** — to let you sign in. Legal basis: performance of
  the contract.
- **Your questions, the answers, and your notebook** — to provide the service. Legal basis:
  performance of the contract.
- **Your consents** — which version of which document you accepted, and when. Legal basis:
  our legitimate interest in being able to prove it.

## 3. Who else sees it

Your questions and the pages found for them are sent to our language-model provider
(DeepSeek) and our search provider (Jina) to produce an answer. Email is delivered by our mail
provider. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

## 4. How long we keep it

Your threads and notebook are kept until you delete your account; your account details are
then anonymised.

## 5. Your rights

You can export your account data, or ask for your account to be anonymised, from within the
application. Consent records survive anonymisation, because they are evidence we are legally
required to keep.
